# QUEM CONHECE, CONHECE.

**Notas de campo de Cotegipe, Bahia: uma cachaça que a região inteira conhece e um engenho que só se acha por indicação.**

> No oeste baiano, a Cotegipana está na prateleira das cachaçarias. O engenho de onde ela sai fica no fim de 70 quilômetros de chão, e a porteira se abre por conhecido.

`Field Research` · `2026-11-02` · `/pt/editorial/if-you-know-you-know`

---

`BLOCO GRUDADO NA TELA`

_COTEGIPE, BAHIA, BRASIL_

> Eu conhecia a garrafa. Nunca tinha visto o engenho.

Minha tia me chamou para ir a Cotegipe, viagem de família.
De Barreiras até lá são 102 quilômetros.

Cotegipe é uma cidade pequena, uns 13 mil habitantes.
Mas era São João, e São João no interior da Bahia é sempre bem recebido.
Fecharam a praça, a prefeitura contratou os artistas e a festa foi na rua.

## O CERRADO

`IMAGEM`

![Campo aberto e cerca de arame no cerrado, uma árvore sozinha no horizonte sob o céu largo.](/images/editorial/cotegipe/cerrado-cerca.webp)



_TAVARÍSTICO / .TBF. inc_

Cotegipe fica no oeste baiano e não se parece com ele.
Em 2024 o município colheu 1.800 hectares de sorgo, 1.308 de milho e 241 de soja, segundo o IBGE.
No vizinho São Desidério, a soja passou de 508 mil hectares.

Cana, em Cotegipe, são poucas dezenas de hectares.
**Cotegipe é o pedaço do oeste que a soja não ocupou.**

## A GARRAFA

A cachaça de lá eu conhecia desde a pré-adolescência. Na região, ela é muito conhecida.
O engenho não vende para quem bebe.
A Cotegipana sai de lá para parceiros comerciais, cachaçarias e lojas escolhidas, e é na prateleira deles que a gente compra.

Mais uns 70 quilômetros de chão, rasgando o sertão baiano atrás do engenho.

Quase nos perdemos algumas vezes.
O pessoal tinha avisado que o acesso era difícil por causa do canavial.

# IF YOU KNOW, YOU KNOW.

**Field notes from Cotegipe, Bahia: a cachaça the whole region knows, and a mill you only find through someone.**

> In western Bahia, Cotegipana sits on the shelves of the local cachaça bars. The mill it comes from is at the end of 70 kilometres of dirt road, and the gate opens through people you know.

`Field Research` · `2026-11-02` · `/en/editorial/if-you-know-you-know`

---

`BLOCO GRUDADO NA TELA`

_COTEGIPE, BAHIA, BRAZIL_

> I knew the bottle. I had never seen the mill.

My aunt asked me to come to Cotegipe. A family trip.
From Barreiras it is 102 kilometres.

Cotegipe is a small town, some 13,000 people.
But it was São João, and in inland Bahia São João is always welcome.
They closed off the square, the town hall hired the acts, and the party was out in the street.

## THE CERRADO

`IMAGEM`

![Open field and a wire fence in the cerrado, a lone tree on the horizon under a wide sky.](/images/editorial/cotegipe/cerrado-cerca.webp)



_TAVARÍSTICO / .TBF. inc_

Cotegipe sits in western Bahia and looks nothing like it.
In 2024 the municipality harvested 1,800 hectares of sorghum, 1,308 of corn and 241 of soy, according to IBGE.
Next door in São Desidério, soy passed 508,000 hectares.

Cane, in Cotegipe, comes to a few dozen hectares.
**Cotegipe is the piece of the west that soy never took.**

## THE BOTTLE

I had known the cachaça from there since I was a kid. In the region, it is well known.
The mill does not sell to drinkers.
Cotegipana leaves for trade partners, cachaça bars and a few chosen shops, and that is where you buy it.

`IMAGEM`

![Packs of little rapadura from the mill, each with a yellow label, a barcode and a nutrition table.](/images/editorial/cotegipe/rapadurinha.webp)



_TAVARÍSTICO / .TBF. inc_

The little rapadura from the same mill has a barcode and a nutrition table.
It was made to travel to someone else’s counter.

## THE GATE

The next day, a Sunday, we hit the road again.
Another 70 kilometres or so of dirt, tearing through the Bahian backlands, looking for the mill.

We nearly got lost a few times.
People had warned us it was hard to reach, because of the cane.

`FRASE GIGANTE, TELA INTEIRA`

> # The cane hides the way in.

`IMAGEM`

![Tall sugarcane against the light, dry straw on the ground.](/images/editorial/cotegipe/cana-contra-luz.webp)



_TAVARÍSTICO / .TBF. inc_

`IMAGEM`

![The ENGENHO arrow sign on its wooden post, upright against the clouds.](/images/editorial/cotegipe/placa-em-pe.webp)

The sign shows up before the mill does.

_TAVARÍSTICO / .TBF. inc_

**An address is not enough.**
We only got there, and were only let in, because my family had friends who knew the owner.

## THE HOUSE

The owner showed us everything, from the very beginning.
At the end he invited us to see his house, right next to where he makes it all.

He thanked us for coming.
He was glad that people who already knew what he made wanted to see how it was made.

A story in a zine is also a way of saying something is worth it. I know that.

`IMAGEM`

![The low sun in the car’s side mirror on the road back, trees against the sky.](/images/editorial/cotegipe/retrovisor.webp)

The way back, late afternoon.

_TAVARÍSTICO / .TBF. inc_

`FECHO`

> Around there, if you know, you know. To reach the place Cotegipana comes from, you still have to know someone.
