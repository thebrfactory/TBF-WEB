**Notas de campo de Posse, Goiás: uma procissão, uma batalha a cavalo entre mouros e cristãos, e um carro rebaixado com o grave alto.**

> No interior de Goiás, uma festa católica de raiz europeia divide o campo com som feito para eletrofunk. Ninguém combinou de separá-los.

`BLOCO GRUDADO NA TELA`

_POSSE, GOIÁS, BRASIL_

> Em Posse, Goiás, a história não fica no passado.

Cheguei em 24 de maio, para a festa do Divino Espírito Santo.
Vim a convite de família. Meu primo era folião, um dos que põem a Folia do Divino de pé.

Grupos percorrem a cidade carregando a bandeira do Divino, a pomba branca.
As pessoas abrem a casa. Cantam, rezam, visitam.

Mas Posse não termina na oração.

A visita vira espetáculo. O campo recebe cavalos, figurinos, coreografia.
A história entra pelas Cavalhadas, a encenação da batalha entre mouros e cristãos, de origem europeia, há muito brasileira.

Por um minuto passa por peça histórica.
Até que um carro rebaixado aparece.

# THE HOLY, THE HORSEPOWER & THE CLOWN.

**Field notes from Posse, Goiás: a procession, a horseback battle between Moors and Christians, and a lowered car with the bass up.**

> In inland Goiás, a Catholic feast with European roots shares a field with sound systems built for eletrofunk. Nobody agreed to keep them apart.

`Field Research` · `2026-09-10` · `/en/editorial/brazilian-country-side`

---

`BLOCO GRUDADO NA TELA`

_POSSE, GOIÁS, BRAZIL_

> In Posse, Goiás, history does not stay in the past.

I got there on May 24th, for the feast of the Holy Spirit.
Family invite. My cousin was a folião, one of those who put the Folia do Divino together.

Groups walk the city carrying a flag with the Holy Spirit on it, a white dove.
People open the house. They sing, they pray, they visit.

But Posse does not end at prayer.

The visit turns into spectacle. The field fills with horses, costumes, choreography.
History comes in through the Cavalhadas, the staged battle between Moors and Christians, European in origin, long since Brazilian.

For a minute it passes for a historical play.
Until a lowered car shows up.

`FRASE GIGANTE, TELA INTEIRA`

> # And the bass gets there before the car does.

## PRODUCTION

`IMAGEM`

![An accordion player in a cap, mid tune, inside the crowd of the feast.](/images/editorial/country-side/02-sanfona.webp)

The feast is made by hand, one player at a time.

_TAVARÍSTICO / .TBF. inc_

Tradition here is not being preserved.
**It is being produced.**

In 2026 the Goiás state government put R$ 5 million into the Cavalhadas circuit. Sixteen towns. Posse is one of them.

Tradition needs logistics.
Heritage needs a budget.

**Cavalhada in Posse has two producers.**
Public money funds the circuit.
The young bring the mask, the car and the sound.

On the day, all of it disappears behind the spectacle.

Then the characters. Among them, the clowns.

Clown is not a big enough word for it.
Masked and colorful, they hold a place of their own in the Cavalhadas, part of the tradition and belonging to something else at once.

- To childhood.

The younger ones get it without explanation.
They put on the mask, play with their own image. The costume becomes a way of talking to everyone else.

The Cavalhadas too.

In Posse it happens in the Cerrado.

With horses, families and young people from there.
